content: To determine how much Weng earned for babysitting, we need to calculate her earnings based on her hourly rate and the time she worked.
---
### Step 1: Convert Minutes to Hours

Weng worked for **50 minutes**, and we know that **1 hour = 60 minutes**. To express her work time in hours:
$$
\text{Hours worked} = \frac{50}{60} = \frac{5}{6} \text{ hours}
$$
---
### Step 2: Calculate Earnings

Weng earns **$12 per hour**. So, her earnings for the work time are:
$$
\text{Earnings} = 12 \times \frac{5}{6} = \frac{60}{6} = 10
$$
---
### Step 3: Verification

To double-check, we can calculate her **per-minute rate**:
$$
\text{Per-minute rate} = \frac{12}{60} = 0.2 \text{ dollars per minute}
$$
Then, for **50 minutes**:
$$
\text{Earnings} = 0.2 \times 50 = 10
$$

This confirms the calculation.
---
### Final Answer
$$
\boxed{10}
$$
