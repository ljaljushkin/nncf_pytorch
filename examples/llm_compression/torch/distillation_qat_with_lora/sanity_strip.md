content: To determine how much Weng earned for her 50-minute babysitting session, we need to convert the time from minutes to hours and then apply the hourly rate.
---
### Step 1: Convert Minutes to Hours
A full hour consists of 60 minutes. Therefore, 50 minutes is:
$$
\frac{50}{60} = \frac{1}{6} \text{ hour} \approx 0.8333 \text{ hours}
$$
---
### Step 2: Apply the Hourly Rate
Weng earns $12 per hour. So, for 50 minutes, she earns:

$$
12 \times \left(\frac{50}{60}\right) = 12 \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{12}{6} = 10
$$

---
### Step 3: Verify with Alternate Method
We can also calculate the per-minute rate:
$$
\frac{12}{60} = 0.2 \text{ dollars per minute}
$$
Then, for 50 minutes:
$$
50 \times 0.2 = 10 \text{ dollars}
$$
---
### Final Answer
All methods confirm that Weng earned:
$$
\boxed{10}
$$
dollars for her 50-minute babysitting session.
