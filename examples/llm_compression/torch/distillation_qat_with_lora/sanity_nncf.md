content: Weng earns $12 per hour for babysitting. She worked 50 minutes, which is equivalent to $\frac{50}{60} = \frac{5}{6}$ hours.

To calculate her earnings, we multiply her hourly rate by the number of hours she worked:
$$
12 \times \frac{5}{6} = \frac{60}{6} = 10
$$

Alternatively, we can calculate her earnings per minute by dividing her hourly rate by 60:
$$
\frac{12}{60} = 0.2 \text{ dollars per minute}
$$
Then, multiply by the number of minutes she worked:
$$
0.2 \times 50 = 10
$$

Both methods confirm that Weng earned $\boxed{10}$ dollars.
